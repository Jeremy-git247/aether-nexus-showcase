# Proposed recruiter walkthrough

**Length:** About three minutes. **Format:** Private screen share or an approved recording. **Status:** Plan only; no recording is included.

| Time | Show | Explain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:30 | Mission Control with synthetic missions | One dashboard tracks two kinds of long-running creative workflows. |
| 0:30–1:15 | A prepared video mission and its artifact list | Background work produces reviewable outputs and progress events. |
| 1:15–1:45 | A prepared game build and QA summary | Game packaging combines structured planning, generation, and static checks; Studio play-testing remains necessary. |
| 1:45–2:20 | A safe, prepared interrupted mission | Saved progress supports recovery; discuss what cannot safely be retried. |
| 2:20–3:00 | Artifact review and the architecture diagram | Explain the separation between generation, human inspection, and publishing. Stop before publishing. |

Use a separate demo environment with synthetic names and prepared outputs. Keep publishing disabled and avoid launching paid work during the presentation. Do not show the Connections page, raw logs, account identifiers, private prompts, local paths, or live customer/content data.

If a live environment is unavailable, present the architecture and engineering notes and label the session as a design walkthrough. Do not imply that screenshots or a scripted narrative prove live provider success.
